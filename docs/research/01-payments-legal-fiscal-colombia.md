# Research: payments and Colombian legal/fiscal requirements

> Research notes, September 2026. Fees are approximate and quoted from the pages listed at the end; items marked **verify** come from secondary sources or pages that could not be fetched (sic.gov.co, funcionpublica.gov.co, suin-juriscol). Confirm fiscal items with an accountant before launch.

## A. Recommended decisions

**MVP gateway: Wompi (Bancolombia).** Covers the methods that convert in Colombia: cards (Visa/MC, tokenized), PSE, Nequi push, Botón Bancolombia, Bancolombia QR, Daviplata (OTP), cash at 15,000+ Bancolombia corresponsales, BNPL Bancolombia (4 cuotas 0%, orders ≥ $100,000), SU+ PAY and Puntos Colombia. Web Checkout/Widget keeps the store in PCI SAQ A. Clean REST API, real sandbox (`sandbox.wompi.co/v1`, `pub_test_/prv_test_` keys), signed webhooks, an integrity signature on amounts, and "acceptance tokens" that bake T&C + Habeas Data consent into every API transaction. Fees quoted by secondary sources at ~2.65% + $700 + IVA per approved transaction, next-business-day settlement (verify on wompi.co; official tariff page blocked). Limits: COP only, Colombia only, no Efecty/Baloto network, 3DS support not confirmed in docs (verify).

**Second option: Mercado Pago** (Checkout Pro to launch fast; Checkout API/Bricks for on-site UX). Cards, PSE, Efecty cash, MP wallet, 3DS 2.0, official published fees (3.29% + $800 + IVA instant settlement; 2.99% at 7 days; 2.79% at 14 days), HMAC-signed webhooks with simulator, test accounts/cards. Alternate second: **PayU (now Rapyd)** if you need Baloto/Su Red/Bre-B QR, installments (Codensa up to 48) or two-step auth/capture (needs PayU approval). **ePayco** only if Davivienda-centric (2.64% + $690 + IVA).

**BNPL:** add **Addi** as a redirect adapter later (OAuth2 client credentials, `POST /v1/online-applications`, callback with APPROVED/REJECTED/ABANDONED, 50,000–10,000,000 COP, commission deducted from disbursement). Sistecrédito works by buying your invoice; no public developer docs found (verify via portal aliados).

**Not for MVP:** Stripe is not available to Colombia-based entities (stripe.com/global lists only Brazil and Mexico in LatAm; workaround = foreign entity via Atlas). PayPal only for USD/international buyers (5.40% + fixed, 4.5% FX markup). dLocal targets global enterprises entering Colombia (no public pricing); dLocal Go SME coverage for Colombia unconfirmed (verify).

**Invoicing:** the store will be responsable de IVA, so factura electrónica is mandatory. Any sale > 5 UVT ($261,870 in 2026, UVT $52,374) cannot go on a POS ticket, and consoles exceed that, so issue a **factura electrónica de venta for every order** on APPROVED, nota crédito on refund/retracto/reversión, via a proveedor tecnológico API (Factus V2 OAuth2 is the most developer-oriented; Alegra, Siigo, Dataico, Matias, Facturatech also expose APIs). Checkout must capture buyer name + cédula/NIT + email (also needed by PSE and Addi). Evaluate Régimen Simple (RST) with an accountant.

**Legal must-haves (details in D):** seller identity block, tax-inclusive COP prices, order summary + express acceptance, order acknowledgement email, retracto and reversión policies and flows, garantía per SKU/service, repair intake receipt (Art. 18), PQR channel with radicado and 15-business-day SLA, age gate, Habeas Data authorization + política + aviso, cookie notice, marketing-contact rules (Ley 2300).

## C. Payment domain design

**Aggregates:** `Order` (owns stock reservation), `Payment` (one per order, canonical state), `PaymentAttempt` (one per provider transaction, provider id + raw status), `ProviderEvent` (raw webhook, unique on provider+event id), `Refund`, `Dispute`.

**Canonical states:** `CREATED -> PENDING -> APPROVED | DECLINED | EXPIRED | VOIDED`; `APPROVED -> PARTIALLY_REFUNDED -> REFUNDED`; `APPROVED -> DISPUTE_OPEN -> DISPUTE_WON | DISPUTE_LOST`. Optional `AUTHORIZED -> CAPTURED` only for providers with two-step (PayU on approval; Stripe `requires_capture`). Map provider statuses in each adapter (Wompi PENDING/APPROVED/DECLINED/VOIDED/ERROR; PayU 4/6/5/7; MP approved/pending/in_process/rejected/cancelled/refunded/charged_back; ePayco 1–11; dLocal 100–700). Final states win over PENDING; transitions are validated by the state machine and rejected when duplicate (idempotent by construction).

**Webhook pipeline:** verify signature with provider secret -> insert `ProviderEvent` (unique index dedupes retries) -> return 200 immediately -> process from an outbox/queue -> **re-fetch the transaction from the provider API** before applying a final state (Wompi: "never use the redirect as validation"; MP and dLocal say the same). Compare amount/currency/reference against the order before approving. Redirect/response pages only render UI.

**Idempotency:** provider `reference` = `orderId-attemptN`, unique per attempt; send `X-Idempotency-Key` (MP) or `Idempotency-Key` (Stripe pattern, 24 h retention, same key + different params = error). Optimistic locking (`@Version`) on `Payment`.

**Reconciliation:** scheduler polls attempts still PENDING past the method's expected window; daily job matches provider settlement report (net of fees and retenciones) against APPROVED payments and flags mismatches; retenciones (1.5% renta, reteIVA, reteICA) are recorded per settlement for the accountant.

**Async methods and stock:** reservation TTL per method: cards/Nequi 10–30 min; PSE hold until provider expiry (often minutes, can be hours; verify per provider); cash references 1–3 days (set `expiration-time` in Wompi, `date_of_expiration` in MP/PayU) with the reservation aligned to that expiry. On PENDING extend to provider expiry; release on DECLINED/EXPIRED/VOIDED; late APPROVED after release -> re-reserve if stock exists, else auto-refund. Repair services reserve a technician slot, not stock.

**Refunds/disputes:** partial refunds by order line (mixed product + service orders); each refund emits a nota crédito. Reversión de pago (Ley 1480 Art. 51) is a legal dispute path through the issuer, modelled as `Dispute(reason=REVERSION)` alongside chargebacks (MP `topic_chargebacks_wh`, dLocal chargebacks). Keep evidence: acceptance log, T&C version, delivery proof, repair receipt.

**PCI/3DS/fraud:** stay SAQ A with hosted widget/redirect/iframe; store only brand, last4, expiry. A merchant-controlled card form posting to gateway JS drops you to SAQ A-EP; direct PAN to API = SAQ D. PCI DSS v4.0.1 changed SAQ A eligibility in 2025 (script-attack criterion), verify on PCI SSC. 3DS via MP (2.0) and PayU; Wompi verify. Basic fraud rules: velocity per cédula/email/IP, high-value console orders to manual review, gateway antifraud state (ePayco "Antifraude").

**Money/currency:** `Money(long minorUnits, Currency, int scale)`; COP effectively 0 decimals (dLocal COP zero-decimal; Wompi `amount_in_cents` integer; Stripe treats COP as two-decimal). Keep a per-provider amount codec in the adapter. Round HALF_UP per line, store presentment currency and an FX snapshot on the order; settle in COP.

**Adapter contract (sketch):**

```java
interface PaymentProvider {
  ProviderId id(); Set<PaymentMethod> methods(); Set<Capability> capabilities(); // REFUND_PARTIAL, VOID, TWO_STEP
  CheckoutSession createCheckout(PaymentRequest r);      // redirectUrl or widget params + signature
  ProviderTransaction fetch(String providerTxId);
  RefundResult refund(String providerTxId, Money amount, String idempotencyKey);
  ProviderEvent parseAndVerify(HttpHeaders h, byte[] body); // throws on bad signature
}
```

Tax engine: `TaxCalculator.calculate(TaxContext{sellerCountry, shipTo, taxClass, customerType, date}) -> TaxLines{rate, base, amount, jurisdiction, inclusive}`; `ColombiaIvaCalculator` (19/5/0/excluido by tax class) first, Stripe Tax/Avalara adapters later (same registration -> calculate -> file cycle).

## E. Glossary

PSE: ACH Colombia bank-transfer rail, async. Nequi / Daviplata: mobile wallets (Bancolombia / Davivienda). Botón Bancolombia: account-debit button. Bre-B: Banco de la República instant-payment system with llaves and QR (full operation Oct 2025). Efecty, Baloto, Su Red, Puntored, Gana: cash-payment networks via reference codes. Corresponsal bancario: bank agent point for cash. BNPL: buy-now-pay-later installments. Addi / Sistecrédito: Colombian BNPL/credit providers. Wompi, Mercado Pago, PayU/Rapyd, ePayco, dLocal, PayPal, Stripe: gateways (Rapyd bought PayU LatAm in 2025). Checkout Pro / Checkout API (Bricks): MP redirect vs on-site products. WebCheckout: PayU hosted page. Hosted checkout / widget / redirect: card data captured by the provider. Tokenization: PAN replaced by a token. Acceptance token: Wompi JWT proving T&C/Habeas Data consent. Integrity signature: hash binding reference+amount+currency. Webhook: provider-to-server event POST. HMAC / checksum: signature scheme to verify webhooks. Idempotency key: client key that makes retries safe. Long polling: repeated status GET until final state. Reconciliation: matching provider reports with your ledger. Settlement: funds transfer to your bank, net of fees. Retenciones: withholdings deducted at settlement. Chargeback / dispute: issuer-initiated reversal and its case. Reversión del pago: Ley 1480 Art. 51 statutory reversal. Retracto: 5-business-day withdrawal right (Art. 47). Garantía legal: statutory warranty (Arts. 7-18). PQR: peticiones, quejas y reclamos channel. Contrato de adhesión: pre-drafted T&C. Acuse de recibo / mensaje de datos / firma digital: Ley 527 e-commerce concepts. PCI DSS: card-security standard; SAQ A / A-EP / D: self-assessment levels. PAN: card number. 3-D Secure: cardholder authentication. Antifraude: gateway fraud screening. Payment state machine: canonical status transitions. Authorization vs capture / void: hold, take, cancel. Partial refund: refund by line. Reservation TTL: stock hold expiry. Outbox: transactional event queue. Minor units: amounts as integers. Presentment vs settlement currency: shown vs paid-out currency. FX snapshot: stored exchange rate. Tax engine / tax class: rate resolver and product tax category. IVA: 19% general, 5%, 0% exento, excluido. Base gravable: taxable base. UVT: tax value unit ($52,374 in 2026). RUT / NIT: tax registry and id. DIAN: tax authority. SIC: consumer/data regulator. Factura electrónica de venta: DIAN-validated invoice. Documento equivalente POS electrónico: ticket for sales ≤ 5 UVT. CUFE / CUDE: unique document codes. RADIAN: invoice-as-security registry. Nota crédito: invoice correction/refund document. Proveedor tecnológico: DIAN-authorized invoicing provider (Factus, Alegra, Siigo, Dataico, Matias, Facturatech). Responsable de IVA: VAT-registered seller. RST: simplified unified tax regime. ReteIVA / ReteICA: VAT and municipal withholdings. Ley 1480: consumer statute. Ley 1581 / Decreto 1377: data protection and its regulation. RNBD: national database registry. Ley 2300: contact hours/consent law. Ley 527: e-commerce/data-message law. Decreto 587/2016: payment-reversal regulation. Aviso de privacidad / política de tratamiento: privacy notice / policy. MoR: merchant of record (cross-border model, e.g., dLocal).
